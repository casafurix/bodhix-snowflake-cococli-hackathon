# Product Experience Direction

## Subject and job

The product is **TrialOps Evidence Desk**, used by clinical-trial coordinators to resolve the next evidence-backed operational decision. The primary screen's single job is to turn a pre-screening result into a safe, human-reviewed action.

## Visual system

- Protocol ink: `#10233B`
- Evidence blue: `#1D5A85`
- Verified teal: `#23877E`
- Review amber: `#D7902F`
- Deviation red: `#B84B52`
- Lab paper: `#F4F7F9`

Headings use the native `Iowan Old Style`/Georgia stack to evoke a reviewed protocol without looking like a generic SaaS dashboard. Body and utility text use `Avenir Next`/system sans for fast operational scanning.

## Layout and signature

The navigation separates Coordinator, Hospital Operations, Sponsor Portfolio, and Scenario Lab. The memorable element is the **evidence rail**: every patient decision is visually joined to a protocol clause, a patient source, and a review state. It borrows the margin-annotation language of clinical protocols and makes explainability part of the interface structure rather than a tooltip.

Forecasting and compliance use the same evidence pattern, so predictions always show their contributing measures and operational recommendations.
